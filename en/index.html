GratCheck
Effective date: August 26, 2026

Thank you for using GratCheck. We value your privacy and aim to clearly explain what data is used by the application, why it is used, and what choices are available to you.

This Privacy Policy describes how data is handled when you use GratCheck.

1. General Information

GratCheck is an application for tracking tips, work rates, work schedules, and viewing earnings statistics.

The application does not require you to create a user account.

The main data created by the user is stored locally on the user's device.

💡 Important: Your tip records, work shifts, work rates, notes, and earnings statistics are stored locally on your device and are not sent to the application's servers.

2. User Data

All data that you create or enter into the application is stored locally on your device.

This may include:

  • tip records;
  • work rates;
  • work shifts and work schedules;
  • notes;
  • data required to calculate earnings statistics;
  • application settings.

We do not have access to the contents of your tip records, work shifts, work rates, notes, or earnings statistics.

3. Notifications

The application may send local notifications reminding you to record your tips for a workday.

Notifications — are used only to remind you to record your tips for a workday.

Notifications are not used for advertising or marketing messages.

The application uses the operating system's notification mechanisms to provide these reminders.

You can disable notifications at any time:

  • in the application settings;
  • in the system settings of your device.

Disabling notifications does not affect other application features and does not delete previously saved user data.

4. Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics, provided by Google, to analyze application usage and improve the application.

Firebase Analytics may automatically process technical and statistical information related to the use of the application, such as:

  • device type and model;
  • operating system version;
  • application version;
  • information about interactions with application features;
  • technical identifiers used by the service;
  • other technical information required for analytics.

This information is used to analyze application usage, evaluate features, and improve the application.

5. Crash Diagnostics

We use Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google, to detect, analyze, and resolve technical problems and application crashes.

Crashlytics may automatically collect technical information related to application crashes, including:

  • device model and type;
  • operating system version;
  • application version;
  • information about the crash;
  • technical information about the application's state at the time of the crash;
  • other information required for diagnostics.

This information is used to detect errors, analyze crashes, improve stability, and improve the quality of the application.

Crashlytics is not used to store user records of tips, work shifts, work rates, notes, or earnings statistics.

6. Advertising

GratCheck displays advertisements. The application uses Yandex advertising services to display advertisements.

Advertising services may automatically process technical information required to display advertisements and measure advertising performance.

This information may include:

  • the device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID), if available;
  • device information;
  • device model;
  • operating system version;
  • technical information about the advertising SDK;
  • information about interactions with advertisements;
  • information required to measure advertising performance.

Data processing by the advertising service is carried out in accordance with the privacy policy of the relevant provider.

Yandex Privacy Policy: https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/

7. Personalized Advertising

After reviewing this Privacy Policy during the first launch of the application, you will be separately asked whether you allow personalized advertising.

Allow personalized advertising — advertising services may use available advertising data to provide more relevant advertisements.
Don't allow — personalized advertising based on this choice is not permitted.

Permission for personalized advertising is a separate user choice and is not required to use the main features of the application.

If you do not allow personalized advertising, advertisements may still be displayed. In this case, non-personalized or contextual advertising may be shown.

💡 Important: Refusing personalized advertising does not mean that all advertising will be disabled.

8. Storage of Your Advertising Choice

Your choice regarding personalized advertising is stored locally on your device.

The application stores whether you have allowed or refused personalized advertising.

This choice is not sent to the application's own servers.

9. Third-Party Services

The application uses third-party services and software components for certain features:

  • Firebase Analytics — application usage analytics;
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash diagnostics;
  • Yandex Mobile Ads — displaying advertisements.

Data processing by these providers is carried out in accordance with their respective privacy policies and terms of use.

10. Sharing of Data with Third Parties

We do not sell or share the contents of your tip records, work shifts, work rates, notes, or earnings statistics with third parties.

However, third-party SDKs integrated into the application may independently process technical information required to provide their respective services.

11. Data Storage and Deletion

The main user data of the application is stored locally on the user's device.

This includes:

  • tips;
  • work rates;
  • work shifts;
  • work schedules;
  • notes;
  • earnings statistics;
  • application settings;
  • the user's personalized advertising choice.

You can delete locally stored application data using the features provided by your operating system.

Technical data collected by third-party services is stored and deleted by the respective providers in accordance with their data retention policies.

12. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data processed by the application.

The main user data is stored directly on the user's device and is not transmitted to the application's servers.

The security of locally stored data also depends on the security settings of the user's device.

13. Your Rights and Choices

You may:

  • use the application without creating an account;
  • disable notifications in the application;
  • disable notifications in the system settings;
  • refuse personalized advertising;
  • manage advertising settings through the operating system, where such options are provided by the platform;
  • delete locally stored application data using the operating system.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published in the application and/or on the Privacy Policy webpage.

The effective date is shown at the beginning of this Privacy Policy.
