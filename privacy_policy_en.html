Thank you for using Tiplens. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your data. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. General Information

Tiplens is a tips and shift management application. All your tips data and shifts are stored locally on your device.

💡 Important: All your tips and shifts data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

2. Data Collection and Usage

2.1 User Data

All notes, reminders, and user content you create are stored exclusively on your device and are not transmitted to our servers or third parties.

2.2 Notifications

To send scheduled reminders and app update notifications, the app uses push services. This process involves generating and using a device token - a unique identifier required to deliver messages to your device.

Notifications — to send you reminders according to your work schedule and receive push notifications about possible app updates.

2.3 Analytics and App Improvement

To analyze crashes and improve app stability, we collect anonymous crash logs and performance data. This data does not contain personal information and is used solely for diagnostics and improvement.

2.4 Advertising

The app displays advertising banners. For ad display and personalization, advertising networks may automatically collect and process the following data:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • Device information (device type, OS version)
  • Data on interaction with ads

This data collection is carried out by advertising partners in accordance with their privacy policies. The app uses Yandex Advertising Network.

Yandex Privacy Policy: https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/

3. Your Consent

By using the app, you consent to the processing of technical data as described in this policy. You can accept or decline this consent when first launching the app.

4. Data Storage

All your user data (tips, shifts, notes) is stored locally. Technical data may be processed by our partners for the purposes described above.

5. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the data we handle. However, please be aware that the security of data stored locally on your device also depends on your device's settings.

6. Changes to the Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. All changes will be posted within the app. By continuing to use the app after changes are made, you accept the updated policy.
